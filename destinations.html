Popular Destinations

From volcano slopes to hidden island coves, across all six provinces.

All Bicol Albay Camarines Norte Camarines Sur Catanduanes Masbate Sorsogon
Volcano

Mayon Volcano

Legazpi City & Daraga, Albay

Famous for its almost perfect cone, Mayon is the most active volcano in the Philippines. It looks best at sunrise from Cagsawa or Lignon Hill. Always follow PHIVOLCS advisories since the danger zone is restricted.

Heritage

Cagsawa Ruins

Daraga, Albay

The lone bell tower of Cagsawa stands as a reminder of the 1814 eruption. Its stones frame Mayon in one of the most photographed views in the country. The park is open all day with local snacks nearby.

Viewpoint

Lignon Hill Nature Park

Legazpi City, Albay

A hilltop park with a wide view of Mayon, the city, and Albay Gulf. Visitors can walk the trails or ride the zipline. Go early in the morning when the clouds are thin.

Beach

Calaguas Islands

Vinzons, Camarines Norte

Calaguas is known for Mahabang Buhangin, a long stretch of powdery white sand and clear water. The islands are best enjoyed with camping and island hopping. Pack light since facilities are basic.

Beach

Bagasbas Beach

Daet, Camarines Norte

A long gray-sand beach popular with surfers and sunset watchers. Beginners can take lessons while locals grill fresh seafood nearby. It is close to Daet town center.

Island

Caramoan Peninsula

Caramoan, Camarines Sur

Limestone cliffs, hidden lagoons, and white-sand coves make Caramoan a favorite for island hopping. The area has hosted international reality shows. Boat rides start from the town port.

Adventure

Camsur Watersports Complex

Pili, Camarines Sur

CWC is the home of cable wakeboarding in the country. Both beginners and pros can ride on the lake with Mayon-like views in the distance on clear days. It also has pools and a viewing deck.

Forest

Mount Isarog Natural Park

Naga City & nearby towns, Camarines Sur

A forested mountain with trails, waterfalls, and rich wildlife. It is a good place for hikers who want cool air and green scenery. Hire a local guide for longer treks.

Surf

Puraran Beach

Baras, Catanduanes

Puraran is a surfing destination known for its tube waves called the Majestic. Even non-surfers enjoy the wide Pacific view. The surf season is usually from September to March.

Viewpoint

Binurong Point

Baras, Catanduanes

Rolling green hills end in cliffs above the Pacific Ocean. A short trail leads to wide lookouts perfect for sunrise. Wear sturdy shoes because the paths can be slippery.

Island

Ticao Island

Ticao Island, Masbate

Ticao is a quiet island known for marine life, including manta rays in some seasons. Its slow pace and clear waters suit divers and snorkelers. Ask local operators for the best time to visit.

Wildlife

Donsol Whale Sharks

Donsol, Sorsogon

Donsol is known for butanding (whale shark) interactions in a regulated setting. Evenings offer a firefly watching tour along the river. Book with accredited operators.

Forest

Bulusan Volcano Natural Park

Bulusan, Sorsogon

A green park around Bulusan Volcano with a calm crater lake for kayaking and picnics. The area is rich in forest and birdlife. Check for volcano advisories before visiting.

Beach

Subic Beach

Matnog, Sorsogon

A scenic beach with rock formations and clear water, located near the ferry port to Samar. It is a handy stop for travelers moving between islands. Visit on weekdays for fewer crowds.

Bicol Region

Region V, Philippines. Land of volcanoes, forests, and the sea.
