Activities & Experiences

Fire, forest, and flavor. Pick your kind of adventure.

Adventure

Hike and climb, wheel and wave

  • Hike the trails of Mount Isarog
  • Ride an ATV around Mayon’s lava trails
  • Try cable wakeboarding at CWC
  • Surf the waves of Puraran and Bagasbas
  • Zipline at Lignon Hill

Island & Nature

Sea, sand, and starlit rivers

  • Island hop in Caramoan and Calaguas
  • Swim with whale sharks in Donsol (with accredited operators)
  • Watch fireflies glow along the Donsol River
  • Kayak on the Bulusan Lake
  • Snorkel with marine life around Ticao

Food & Flavors

The spicy side of Luzon

  • Taste Bicol Express, pork in chili and coconut milk
  • Try laing, taro leaves in creamy coconut milk
  • Eat pinangat wrapped in gabi leaves
  • Slurp kinalas noodles in Naga City
  • Snack on pili nut sweets and sili ice cream

Culture & Heritage

Faith, festivals, and old stones

  • Visit the Cagsawa bell tower
  • Join the Peñafrancia devotion in Naga City
  • Explore old churches in Daraga and Camalig
  • Shop for abaca crafts in Catanduanes
  • Watch a rodeo in Masbate

Bicol Region

Region V, Philippines. Land of volcanoes, forests, and the sea.
