Travel Tips

  • Best months are usually March to May for dry, sunny weather.
  • Typhoon season is around June to November, so check forecasts.
  • Follow volcano advisories before visiting Mayon or Bulusan.
  • Book whale shark tours with accredited operators only.
  • Bring cash because small towns may not have ATMs.
  • Respect local customs and protect the environment.
